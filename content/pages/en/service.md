## RATHAUSGARAGE (Town Hall Car Park)

Park in the city centre in our RathausGarage with 350 cool parking spaces. Entrance height: 2.20 m, women’s parking spaces on the 1st lower ground floor, follows signs to “1. UG”.

**Phone:** +43 676 848 802 266

**Opening hours:** 7/24

**Day time charges:**\
1 hr € 3.20, each further half hour € 1.60, maximum day rate: € 25.00

**Night time charges (from 7 pm – 7 am):**\
each further half hour or part thereof € 1.00, maximum night rate: € 8.00

In the case of a lost parking ticket, double the full day rate will be charged (€ 50.00)!

Convenient and faster exit from the garage thanks to **automatic license plate recognition**
Monthly collective invoices possible for frequent parkers --> All information **[BOE Comfort Parking](https://www.boe-parking.at/de/boe-comfort-parking)**
