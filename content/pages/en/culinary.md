Visitors to the RathausGalerien are also well catered for: the shopping center has four restaurants, four bars and a bakery. Thanks to their diverse culinary offerings and selection of different drinks, the bars and restaurants are great places to relax after shopping. Small snacks for breaks during the shopping tour are available in the bakery in the galleries, for example. Café 360 on the top floor of the galleries not only offers its guests delicious drinks, but also a stunning view over the city of Innsbruck.

Another highlight are the two hotels, which are directly adjacent to the shopping facilities. They impress with their innovative design and great location in the heart of the city center. Visit the RathausGalerien and store in our diverse stores!
